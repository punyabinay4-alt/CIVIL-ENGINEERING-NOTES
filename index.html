Estimation Methods - True Methods

1. Long Wall Short Wall: Long Wall = c/c + Wall Thickness, Short Wall = c/c - Wall Thickness
2. Centre Line Method: Total Centre Length = Sum of all walls c/c. If Junctions, Deduct (n/2)*B*H where n=junctions
3. Deductions: Brickwork: Full deduction for opening >0.05m³. Plaster: <0.5m² No deduction, 0.5-3m² 50% one side, >3m² 100% both sides
