🧪 CIVIL ENGINEERING LAB

SOM, Fluid Mechanics, Geotechnical and Concrete laboratory formulas.

SOM LAB

1. Tensile Test:
σ = P/A
ε = ΔL/L
E = σ/ε
% Elongation = (Lf − L0)/L0 × 100

2. Compression:
Strength = P/A

3. Impact Test:
Energy is obtained from the machine readings and specimen geometry.

4. Hardness:
BHN = 2P / [πD(D − √(D²−d²))]

5. Torsion:
T/J = τ/R = Gθ/L

6. Beam Deflection:
Simply supported point load = WL³/48EI
Cantilever point load = WL³/3EI

FLUID MECHANICS LAB

Bernoulli equation:
P/γ + V²/2g + Z = Constant

Continuity:
Q = AV

Reynolds Number:
Re = VD/ν

Pipe friction:
hf = fLV²/2gD

Laminar flow:
Re < 2000

Turbulent flow:
Re > 4000

GEOTECHNICAL LAB

Water Content:
w = Water Weight / Dry Soil Weight × 100

Specific Gravity:
G = Weight of soil solids / Equivalent water weight

Plasticity Index:
PI = LL − PL

Coefficient of Uniformity:
Cu = D60/D10

Coefficient of Curvature:
Cc = D30²/(D10×D60)

Direct Shear:
τ = c + σ tanφ

UCS:
qu = P/A

CBR:
CBR = Test Load / Standard Load × 100
