💼 Top 30 Civil Interview Questions

  • Grade of concrete? M5 to M80, M20 = 20 N/mm² characteristic strength in 28 days
  • Curing period? 7 days min, 14 days for PPC, Columns 7 days, Slab 14-21 days
  • Cover in RCC? Slab 20mm, Beam 25-30mm, Column 40mm, Footing 50mm, As per IS 456
  • Unit weight? Cement 1440 kg/m³, Sand 1600 kg/m³, Concrete 2400 kg/m³, Steel 7850 kg/m³, Brick 1600-1920 kg/m³
  • 1 m³ me kitne cement bags? 28.8 Bags (1440/50)
  • Slump test kya hai? Workability of concrete - 75-100mm for Beam/Slab
  • Water cement ratio? 0.4 to 0.6, Kam ratio = Zyada strength
  • Long wall short wall method? Estimation ka method - Wall to Wall measurement
  • BBS full form? Bar Bending Schedule
  • Least count of dumpy level? 5mm, Theodolite 20 seconds
